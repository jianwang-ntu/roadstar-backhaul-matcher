                deck and any number-shaped token that SURVIVES must be on an

                half that fails on a figure nobody remembered to claim; a

                slides and in the script SEPARATELY. The first draft checked the
                concatenation, and a control proved that a disclosure deleted

  Two of those controls are worth naming individually.

  "results JSON moved under a still deck" copies the whole project to a
  temporary tree, moves profit_assignment's mean profit to 9,999, and requires

  can catch build_figures() being quietly turned into a table of constants --
